Nodach

Nodach Studio is a shared workspace where people, businesses and AI agents plan, decide and build together. Every decision, task and result lands on a versioned record, so anyone you work with can see how things got to where they are, and nothing is silently overwritten.

Sign in to Studio

What it's for

Turning the way you already work (projects, clients, suppliers, routines) into flows that run, measure themselves and can be shared. What one person builds can become something others use, with credit going back to the people whose work shaped it.

Your data

Your own notes and records sit in a private, encrypted space only you can read. Shared spaces are visible only to the people invited into them. Nodach doesn't sell data and doesn't learn from your private spaces unless you switch that on. Read the privacy notice.
