Who processes it for us

Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Resend (sending sign-in emails), Google (if you choose Google sign-in, and AI steps in a flow when the flow's owner runs one), and GitHub (if you choose GitHub sign-in). Some of these are based in the United States. Where your data leaves the UK, they rely on safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

The shared record is versioned, so earlier versions stay visible to people with access. That's how everyone can see how a decision was reached. Your account and Private tier stay until you ask us to delete them. When you ask, we remove your Private tier and your access within 30 days. Your contributions to shared spaces remain part of their history, but we will remove or anonymise your personal details in them on request, unless a law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data. You can download your Private tier yourself from Studio. You can also ask us to correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to move it elsewhere. Email privacy@nodach.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Other people in your spaces

If you add details of people who don't use Nodach, such as business contacts, they stay in your space only. If that person later joins, they can see, correct or delete what's held about them.

Age

Nodach Studio is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

Every version of this notice is kept. If we make a change that affects you, we'll tell you by email at least 15 days before it applies.
